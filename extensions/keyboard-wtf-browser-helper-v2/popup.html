Private by design

This helper reads or acts on a page only when you ask Jarvis to control a browser task. It communicates with the keyboard.wtf app on this computer. When AI page understanding is required, the app sends a bounded task snapshot to your configured Gemini browser model.

Privacy policy

Passwords, OTPs, CAPTCHAs, payment, checkout, and account-security steps stay under your control.
