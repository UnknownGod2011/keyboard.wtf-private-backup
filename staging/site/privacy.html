Staging policy

Privacy

This private staging system uses Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication. The backend stores your user ID, email, display name, account status, server-authorised credit ledger, usage totals, purchase status, and registered Jarvis devices or sessions.

AI requests

Only content required for a user-requested AI action is sent through the authenticated keyboard.wtf backend to Vertex AI. Full prompts, selected screen content, and browser content are not written to ordinary application logs. Usage metadata and concise outcome categories are retained for billing, abuse prevention, reconciliation, and support.

Payments

Razorpay Test Mode receives checkout information. keyboard.wtf stores provider order, payment, subscription and event identifiers plus amounts and statuses. Card, bank, UPI credentials, passwords and OTPs are handled by Razorpay and are not collected by keyboard.wtf.

Controls

You can sign out, revoke registered devices, cancel checkout, and request deletion of staging data. Server records required for security, accounting, refunds, chargebacks and audit may be retained as required. Data is not sold or used for advertising, creditworthiness or unrelated profiling.
