Questions, answered

A few useful details.

The essentials about Jarvis, credits, Browser Automation, Smart Writing, and your account.

What can keyboard.wtf do?

Jarvis can answer questions and help you open apps, use selected text, reason about your screen, search, write, dictate, and carry out supported actions across Windows and Chrome.

How do monthly credits work?

Your plan includes provider-cost-backed credits for supported paid AI features. Account & Billing shows your current balance and itemized recent usage from authoritative settled records.

What is Browser Automation?

Browser Automation lets Jarvis complete user-requested, interactive work in your existing Chrome session. It verifies live page state and pauses when your input or a sensitive decision is required.

Does Browser Automation require Chrome?

Yes. The current Windows release uses the keyboard.wtf Chrome Helper from the Chrome Web Store to connect Jarvis to the tab you ask it to control.

What is Smart Writing?

Smart Writing turns natural speech into polished writing while preserving your meaning and tone. It places the final result on the clipboard and inserts it at the original cursor when that target remains available.

How do I manage my subscription?

Open Account & Billing to see your plan, usage, payments, connected devices, and available subscription controls, including scheduling renewal cancellation when your plan supports it.
